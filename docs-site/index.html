Marketplace Workflow

From creative brief to confirmed booking

Built around structured 10-step requirements, rule-based matching, side-by-side proposals, and atomic booking creation.

01

Post Requirement

Customers select category & specialty, upload inspiration photos, choose area, date, mode, and budget range.

02

Rule-Based Matching

VEYRA filters eligible professionals by category, mode, city/radius, and availability and sends structured alerts.

03

Compare & Chat

Customers compare quoted prices, inclusions, exclusions, and portfolios side by side and message directly.

04

Atomic Booking & Review

Accepting a proposal atomically creates a booking, closes competing quotes, and unlocks verified reviews upon completion.

Platform Architecture

Three Role-Protected Portals

Enforced end-to-end by Firebase Authentication, custom claims, Firestore & Storage Security Rules, and server transactions.

Customer Workspace

10-step request wizard with drafts, proposal comparison, shortlisting, favourites, real-time chat, booking reschedules/cancellations, verified reviews, and account deletion.

Professional Studio

12-step resumable onboarding, image/video portfolio manager, service catalogue, weekly & date availability, private verification upload, request feed, proposal builder, and real analytics.

Admin Operations Portal

Server-enforced custom claims (`super_admin`, `operations_admin`, `verification_admin`, `support_admin`, `content_admin`), verification signed URLs, category/CMS editor, moderation, and audit logs.

Connect Netlify or Vercel to launch the full interactive VEYRA app

GitHub Pages hosts this static showcase only. Connect webveyra-ux/veyra on Netlify (using the included netlify.toml) or Vercel and add your Firebase Web + Admin keys from .env.example to activate login, Firestore requests, Storage uploads, real-time chat, and `/admin`.
