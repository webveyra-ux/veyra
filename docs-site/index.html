How VEYRA Works

From inspiration to confirmed booking in four clear steps

Stop messaging dozens of accounts and repeating your brief. Share your requirements once and receive structured quotes.

01

Post Your Requirement

Select your category, upload inspiration photos, and set your city, preferred date, service mode, and budget range.

02

Matched Pros Respond

Suitable professionals in your area review your brief and send itemised proposals with clear inclusions and pricing.

03

Compare & Message

Compare portfolios, experience, inclusions, and schedules side by side, shortlist favourites, and chat directly.

04

Book & Review

Accept your chosen proposal to confirm your booking, track completion, and share a verified booking review.

Admin-Reviewed Verification

Verified badges are awarded only after administrative review of submitted identification and practice credentials.

Address & Contact Privacy

Open requests share only city and locality details. Exact venue addresses remain protected until a booking is confirmed.

Booking-Verified Reviews

Ratings and written reviews come exclusively from completed bookings—ensuring authentic, transparent feedback.
